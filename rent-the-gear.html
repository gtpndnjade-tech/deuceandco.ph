RENTAL TERMS

RATE

Per racket, per day. Beginner ₱180 · Intermediate ₱200 · Advanced ₱240.

INCLUDED

A case and 5 balls with every racket rental.

DEPOSIT

₱200 refundable deposit for rentals of 2 days or more.

PICKUP

South City Homes, Biñan, Laguna. Delivery around Biñan available by quote.

BOOKING

Confirmed by DM on Instagram or Messenger. No membership needed.

LATE RETURNS

One extra day charged per day overdue. Tell us if you’re running behind — we’ll usually work with you.

DAMAGE

Normal string wear and grip sweat are on us. Cracked frames, snapped strings from misuse, and lost balls are charged at repair or replacement cost.

CANCELLATIONS

Free reschedule or full refund up to 24 hours before your slot. Inside 24 hours, one day of rental is forfeited, or we hold it as credit toward a future booking.

WEATHER OR COURT CLOSURE

Full refund or free reschedule, no penalty. We’ll reach out if we see it coming.

NO-SHOWS

Charged in full. First-time no-shows get one pass — just tell us what happened.
