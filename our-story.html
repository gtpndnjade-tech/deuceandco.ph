THE SPORT THAT RAISED HIM.

CHAPTER 01 · PLAZA DILAO, PACO, MANILA

1982.

Plaza Dilao, Paco, Manila. He was a ball boy at the Philippine Columbian Association. Three months picking up balls, watching matches from the edge of a court he wasn’t yet allowed to play on. Tennis found him before he found it.

CHAPTER 02 · HONG KONG

1999.

Seventeen years later, in Hong Kong, he won the Sikap Tennis Cup — Division B, Men’s Singles. Ball boy to champion. The court that raised him, finally his.
